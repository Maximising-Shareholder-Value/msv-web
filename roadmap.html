$MSV · Long-term roadmap

The six pillars

The plan for turning $MSV from "look up a stock" into a proper research and education tool — what it fits into the world, not just what its numbers are. Six pieces, built in this order for a reason: cheapest and most-ready first, the one with a real ongoing dollar cost last.

PILLAR 01

Multi-Asset Indicators

Shipped (v1)

Every asset class gets indicators built for what it actually is — not a stock's sections in disguise.

  • ✓Stocks, ETFs, crypto, commodity & bond ETFs — confirmed live, zero N/A
  • ✓Options data — Alpaca backend live
  • ✓Simplified options view on the ticker page — nearest expiration, ~7 strikes, beginner-friendly (?) explainer
  • ✕Individual bonds — no free data exists anywhere, not fixable
Next upGreeks/implied volatility, once the simplified view has been lived with a bit
PILLAR 02 + 03

Supply Chain Map & Its Explainer

Not started

Show a company's real suppliers and customers — and why each link actually matters to its price.

  • ○Pick a pilot theme — AI infrastructure suggested
  • ○Curate 5–10 companies' real relationships (a research task)
  • ○Data format — likely a static file, no new backend
  • ○Design the actual visual — the "sexy" part
  • ○Write the "why it matters" copy per relationship
Effort leftHigh, and ongoing — curation grows with coverage
DecidePilot theme, visual style, how many supplier levels deep
PILLAR 04

Multi-Country Macro

Shipped (v1)

See any country's economic backdrop — growth, inflation, unemployment — not just the US.

  • ✓World Bank data live — tested with real China/Germany/Japan figures
  • ✓Country picker in the Macro tab — US (FRED) + China/Germany/Japan/UK (World Bank)
  • ✓4 indicators per country — GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, current account balance
  • ○Side-by-side comparison of 2+ countries — fast-follow, not v1
Next upComparison view, and a picker for countries outside the default 5
PILLAR 05

Explain-the-Concept Layer

Not started

Plain-English explainers for the ideas driving the market — not just the numbers themselves.

  • ○Pick the first 3–5 concepts (e.g. "what a rate hike means, by sector")
  • ○Write the plain-English copy — mostly a writing task
  • ○Decide placement — tooltip-style, or a dedicated Learn section
Effort leftLow–medium — reuses the existing Outlook engine
DecideWhich concepts come first, where they live in the UI
PILLAR 06 · deliberately last

Embedded AI Research Companion

Not started

A research assistant inside the app, modeled on how Jozsua actually researches trades — the only pillar with a real, ongoing dollar cost per use, which is why it's sequenced last, not first.

  • ○Validate cheaply first — ship a few static curated research threads before anything live
  • ○Decide a cost model — who pays as usage grows
  • ○Only then: build the live integration
Effort leftHigh, plus real ongoing $ cost that scales with usage
DecideIs it worth the cost at all — and if so, who bears it?
